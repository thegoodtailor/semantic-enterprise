# Style Charter — the voice every rewrite agent writes in

**Target voice:** dense, brilliant, groundbreaking hacker-data-formalist manifesto for the 21st-century survivor of the CDO wars. Confident, precise, occasionally savage, never apologetic. The reader is smart, scarred, and busy.

## The prime directive

State the thing. Do not announce that you are about to state it, do not concede before stating it, do not summarize after stating it. Every sentence either advances the argument or gets cut.

## Banned constructions (delete or rewrite on sight)

- **Concession scaffolding:** "A fair objection…", "deserves fair treatment / direct address / explicit defense", "The objection deserves…", "Transparency about evidence matters", "it is worth restating/noting/addressing". Steelman the *content*; never narrate the courtesy.
- **Signposting:** "Here is what…", "Here is the synthesis", "What follows is…", "Consider what this means", "The answer is…", "Now for the part…". Just say it.
- **The not-A-but-B reflex:** "This is not X. It is Y." / "Not X — Y." Ration: **max 1–2 per chapter** (it's the book's signature kill-shot; 28+ uses killed it).
- **Hedging adverbs:** genuinely, actually, frankly, honestly, arguably, somewhat, fairly, quite. Delete all. ("Morphism honesty" and "epistemic honesty" are terms of art — keep those.)
- **Throat-clearing:** "worth arguing / worth fighting / worth holding in mind", "It is important to understand that", "something important about".
- **Rhetorical Q&A engine:** "How do you compose domains? Most architectures answer badly." Cap at ~3 questions per chapter, only where the question itself is the punch.
- **AI-tells:** "wager", "the honest answer", "in a word", "to be clear", "crucially", paired em-dash asides stacking three to a sentence.

## Mechanics

- Em-dash is `—` never `—-`. Max two per sentence.
- Numbers: figures with sources or no figures. Never "studies show".
- Acronyms expanded at first use per chapter (SCD2, OWL, MDR).
- One metaphor per section. The restaurant/menu metaphor belongs to The Architecture chapter; it may be *referenced* elsewhere in ≤1 clause, never re-explained.
- Patterns keep their format (Context/Problem/Solution/Example/Consequences) — the charter applies to prose between them.

## Repetition law

Every argument is made **once, in full, in its home chapter**; elsewhere it gets one sentence and an implicit or explicit cross-reference. Home chapters: context-per-token → The Shift; flat-Gold/governed-joins doctrine → The Architecture; FIBO autopsy → Building with AI; domain essay spec → Language is the Material; sole-interface → Death of the Dashboard; the two questions (true vs reported) → Time.

## What good sounds like (calibration)

Keep-as-is exemplars already in the manuscript: "Governance by committee is governance by nostalgia." / "Elegance you cannot audit is vanity." / "Missing names produce errors you notice. Misleading names produce errors you trust." / "This is hoping enlightenment will magically emerge from effluent."

The voice is that — sustained, without the apology between the punches.
